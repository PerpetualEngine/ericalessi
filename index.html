Curiosilab

Curiosilab is Eric's five-hour private group lab, held weekly on Koh Phangan for committed practitioners: one small group, one garden studio. Eric comes with material, and the group only explores what it truly needs:

  • Centers of gravity
  • The Axis Syllabus
  • Horizontal and vertical planes, and beyond
  • Ground work
  • Subtle awareness
  • The art of falling
  • Fears, and the mindless state

Every lab opens with two questions: what do you think you really need, and what would you like to become when dancing Contact? From there the group assesses body constitution, sizes, movement backgrounds and current limitations, and makes it all play in favor of more graceful dances. The tools: exercises, sharings, written notes, a jam in the middle of the lab rather than only at the end, and real breaks. Five hours is enough time for something to actually change.

The lab runs from four dancers and caps at eight, so everyone gets direct attention and feedback. The deeper format is the four-week program, one lab a week for a month. That is the ideal vision: to truly transform from the inside out. To join the next lab, write to Eric.

A statement I can't stop returning to:

We treat stability like a virtue. Emma Bigé maintains: equilibrium is where the dance ends, and your partner's real job is to help you lose your vertical. Every time I stop protecting my balance, the dance gets more honest.

  • A question to carry: what becomes possible when balance stops being the goal?
  • A micro/macro practice: stand an arm's length from a wall. Fall toward it, catch with both hands, push back. Ten times, slower each time.
