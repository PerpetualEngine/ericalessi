The five-hour private lab

Every week on Koh Phangan, Eric holds a five-hour private group lab for committed practitioners: one small group, five hours of practice in a garden studio. The format is a lab. Eric comes with material, and the group only explores what it truly needs:

  • Centers of gravity
  • The Axis Syllabus
  • Horizontal and vertical planes, and beyond
  • Ground work
  • Subtle awareness
  • The art of falling
  • Fears, and the mindless state

Every lab opens with two questions: what do you think you really need, and what would you like to become when dancing Contact? From there the group assesses body constitution, sizes, movement backgrounds and current limitations, and makes it all play in favor of more graceful dances. The tools: exercises, sharings, written notes, a jam in the middle of the lab rather than only at the end, and real breaks. Five hours is enough time for something to actually change.

"The most educating workshop I've ever attended." ~ Gaia, Italy, three years dancing

The lab runs from four dancers and caps at eight, so every body gets seen. The deeper format is the four-week program, one lab a week for a month. That is the ideal vision: to truly transform from the inside out. To join the next lab, write to Eric.
