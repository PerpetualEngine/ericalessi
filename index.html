Contact Improvisation · Chiang Mai
Eric Alessi

Contact Improvisation is a dialogue of weight, balance and touch between two moving bodies.

I have practiced Contact Improvisation for seven years and teach it in Chiang Mai, Thailand: weekly classes, jams, and five-hour group immersions for six to eight dancers. My teaching centres on where support comes from: the ground as a partner, the centres of gravity in a duet, and the scores that reverse what the body thinks it knows.

The Practice Journal

Each entry documents one class from both sides. What was taught and why, and what the dancers found, quoted from the recorded closing sharing, with permission and without names. The two are rarely the same, and the distance between them is where the teaching lives.
