Contact Improvisation · Koh Phangan
Eric Alessi

Contact Improvisation is a dialogue of weight, balance and touch between two moving bodies.

I have practiced Contact Improvisation since 2016, started the Zero Angle project in Shanghai in 2017 bringing CI teachers to China, and today live and teach on Koh Phangan, Thailand: weekly classes, jams, and five-hour private labs for groups of up to eight. I teach from one question: where does support come from? The ground, a partner's weight, your own two feet.

The Practice Journal

Each entry documents one class from both sides. What was taught and why, and what the dancers found, quoted from the recorded closing sharing, with permission and without names. The two are rarely the same, and the distance between them is where the teaching lives.
