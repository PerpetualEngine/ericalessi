Contact Improvisation · Chiang Mai

The dance is a dialogue of weight.
I teach both sides of it.

I'm Eric. I've practiced Contact Improvisation for seven years, and I teach it in Chiang Mai: weekly classes, jams, and five-hour group immersions. Everything I learn about teaching it, I publish.

The Practice Journal

Each entry has two halves. What I carried into the room as the teacher, and what the dancers carried out, in their own recorded words from the closing circle. The two are rarely the same, and the distance between them is the most interesting thing I have found in teaching so far.

Classes and immersions

Weekly classes and jams in Chiang Mai, and five-hour group immersions for six to eight dancers who want to go deeper: direct hands-on feedback, long-form scores, and time enough for something to actually change. The current schedule lives in the community group; the Journal announces each immersion.

One email a week. Five items, one minute to read: from the room, a dancer's words, one thing to practise, one thing worth your time, and a question to carry.
